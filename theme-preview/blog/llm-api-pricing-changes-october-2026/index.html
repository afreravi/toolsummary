What moved in LLM API pricing this October

DeepSeek cut output prices by up to 82% on its Flash line, while a handful of smaller models quietly went the other way. Here is the month in five charts and what it means if you are budgeting tokens.

T
The Toolsummary desk
2026-10-27 · 6 min read

Every day this site records what each tracked model costs per million input and output tokens. That log is now long enough to see a month clearly, so this is the first of a recurring price report.

The headline: output tokens got cheaper, fast

DeepSeek's Flash line led the month, with output prices falling by 72–82% on several entries. That is the single largest move we have recorded, and it drags the whole blended cost of a chat-style workload down with it.

A model is only cheap for your workload if its input:output ratio matches how you actually use it. A 20:1 output price is irrelevant if you mostly send, not generate.

The counter-move nobody advertised

Not everything fell. A handful of smaller models raised prices, which is usually a sign of a capacity crunch or a graduation out of a promotional tier. Those are the rows worth a second look before you commit a budget to them.

How to read the tables on this site

Prices are per million tokens, in USD, exactly as the provider publishes them. The blended figure weights input to output 3:1, which is a common chat workload, but your own ratio is the one that matters. Use the comparison pages to put two candidates side by side at your real volumes.

What is coming next

The plan is a weekly digest with the largest movers, and a public history endpoint so you can pull the raw log yourself. Until then, the daily table is the source of truth.
