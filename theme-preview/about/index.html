About Toolsummary

A daily record of what LLM APIs cost, kept in the open because a price is the one fact a language model cannot reconstruct from memory.

T
The Toolsummary desk
Independent · no sponsored placement

What this is

Toolsummary tracks published per-token pricing for LLM and inference APIs and records a snapshot every day. That log is the product: it turns "what does this cost" into a fact with a date on it, rather than a number someone typed over.

How prices are collected

Prices come from provider APIs and public price lists, normalised to USD per million tokens. Anything ambiguous goes to a human review queue instead of onto a page. A rejected row never enters the history.

Independence

No provider pays for placement, and no row is priced differently from any other. If a number is wrong, the fastest fix is the submit form.

Corrections

Spotted a stale price? Send it in with the provider's own pricing page and it will be checked on the next refresh.
