Privacy

The short version: this site collects as little as it can and sells nothing.

What is collected

The public pages are static and read no cookies for tracking. If you use the submit form, the details you enter are used only to verify the row you sent.

What is not done

No data is sold, and no provider is given access to submissions. Email addresses from the submit form are used only to ask a clarifying question.

Analytics

Aggregate, cookie-free page counts only — enough to know which pages are useful, not enough to identify a reader.
